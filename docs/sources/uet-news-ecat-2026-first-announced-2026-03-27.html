UET Announces Combined Entrance Test 2026 for Undergraduate Admissions
Mar 27, 2026 - Apr 3, 2026

The University of Engineering and Technology Lahore (UET Lahore) has announced the conduct of the Combined Entrance Test 2026 (First) for Undergraduate Fall Admissions 2026, scheduled to be held from March 30 to April 2, 2026.
The test will be conducted in multiple sessions across various centres established at UET Lahore, its sub-campuses, and affiliated institutions. A total of 18,500 candidates, including those appearing in the mock test, are expected to participate this year.
The examination will be conducted in a computer-based format, with each session having a duration of 100 minutes. To facilitate candidates and to familiarize them with the testing system, a mock test will be held on Saturday, March 28, 2026, at the same designated centres.
According to the spokesperson of UET Lahore, Rana Tanveer Qasim, all necessary arrangements have been completed to ensure a transparent, smooth, and merit-based conduct of the examination. He further stated that strict compliance with rules and regulations will be ensured at all examination centres.
UET Lahore remains committed to maintaining transparency, merit, and high standards in the admission process, ensuring equal opportunities for all aspiring students across the country.
 Designated Test Centres (Punjab Cities):
University of Engineering and Technology Lahore — Main Campus, Lahore
University of Engineering and Technology Lahore Faisalabad Campus — Faisalabad Campus
University of Engineering and Technology Lahore Narowal Campus — Narowal Campus
Rachna College of Engineering and Technology — Gujranwala
Khawaja Fareed University of Engineering and Information Technology — Rahim Yar Khan
University of Engineering and Technology Taxila — Taxila
University of Gujrat — Gujrat
Bahauddin Zakariya University — Bosan Road, Multan
Islamia University Bahawalpur — Baghdad-ul-Jadeed Campus
NFC Institute of Engineering and Technology Multan — Multan
