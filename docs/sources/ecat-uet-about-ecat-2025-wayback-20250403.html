Why ECAT

As per the directives of the Government of Punjab since 1999, the University of Engineering and Technology (UET) Lahore has been conducting the combined Entrance Test (ECAT) for admission to public sector engineering institutions in Punjab. According to multiple notifications issued by the Pakistan Engineering Council (PEC), this entrance test is mandatory for admission into undergraduate engineering programs in both public and private engineering institutions. This is the first ECAT of the year. In compliance with PEC instructions, applicants will be allowed multiple attempts, and their best score will be considered for determining merit during the admission process. The scores obtained in this ECAT will remain valid for admission to UET for the Fall 2025 semester. The ECAT serves as a common entrance test for admission to B.Sc. Engineering programs across Punjab, as well as for computing and other disciplines where it is applicable for admission to UET Lahore.

Standard of the Test and Subjects to be tested
The Test would be of Intermediate level. It will be offered in the following combinations: (i) Mathematics, Physics and Chemistry, (ii) Mathematics, Physics and Computer Science, (iii) Mathematics, Statistics and Computer Science, (iv) Biology, Physics and Chemistry and (v) Mathematics, Statistics and Physics. The duration of the Test will be hundred minutes. Ten questions will be related to English and the rest, i.e., ninety, will equally be divided amongst the other subjects. Each correct answer is worth four points and for every incorrect answer one point will be deducted. There is no passing threshold for any program.
The candidates should select a combination from one of the above choices, depending on their subjects in Intermediate (or equivalent).
DAE qualified candidates can only choose from the following two options – Physics, Mathematics, English and Chemistry or Physics, Mathematics, English and Computer Science.
Requirement of Photo-ID at the Test Center During ECAT
Failure to present a photo-ID may result in expulsion from the Test Center. Following forms of photo-IDs are valid:
Computerized National Identity Card CNIC/NICOP
Juvenile Card issued by NADRA
Matric/Intermediate certificate carrying photograph
Computerized domicile certificate bearing a photograph
Passport
Statement of Entry in case of “A” Level students.
Who Can Appear in ECAT

Anyone can take the Entrance Test, however, appearance in the Test does not confer the right to apply for admission in the Engineering institutions of the Punjab.

General Instructions
A candidate who misses the entrance test in the allotted center during the allotted time slot will not be permitted to retake the exam.
Since the test is fully automated, no re-checking facility will be provided.
Entrance test token once purchased can’t be refunded / Non transferable at any stage whether utilized or not.
