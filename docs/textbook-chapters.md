# Punjab intermediate textbooks: chapter and topic lists

Checked 9 Nov 2026. These lists support topic tagging in the ECAT app. Only chapter and section titles are recorded here, never book text.

## How these lists were checked

The PCTB/PECTAA site (pctb.punjab.gov.pk) was down for maintenance, so every book was downloaded as a PDF from taleem360.com, which mirrors the board's PDFs. I read the contents page of every book myself. Where the PDF has a text layer I pulled section headings from it; for scanned PDFs I read the chapter-opener pages as images. The PDFs were kept in a temporary folder and are not stored in the repo.

Confidence labels used below:
- **Titles: book** = the chapter titles were read from the book's own contents page.
- **Topics: book** = the topics come from the book's numbered section headings, its "Learning Objectives" / "Student Learning Outcomes" boxes, or its unit introductions.
- **Topics: known** = the titles are confirmed from the book, but the topic list comes from long familiarity with these old PTB books and was not checked page by page. Spot-check these before writing content.
- **Topics: partial** = some topics come from the book and some are inferred; the inferred ones are marked "(inferred)".

Old books carry the "PTB/PCTB, 2002 curriculum" approval line. New books say "based on updated/revised National Curriculum of Pakistan 2023" and are published by PECTAA.

The new Class 12 books continue the Class 11 chapter numbers: Physics 12 runs from chapter 13 to 21, and Chemistry 12 from unit 17 to 33. Maths and CS restart at 1.

## 1. Mathematics

### 1a. OLD Mathematics Part I (Class 11), "Algebra and Trigonometry", PCTB, 14 chapters
Source: book contents page (PCTB, approval letter F.1-5/2002-Maths dated 10 Jun 2005, printed April 2018), PDF from https://www.taleem360.com/11th-class-mathematics-text-book-in-pdf-by-pctb-pxt. The chapter list also matches https://www.mathcity.org/fsc/fsc_part_1_solutions. **Titles: book. Topics: known.**

1. Number Systems: rational and irrational numbers, properties of real numbers, complex numbers (operations, conjugate, modulus), complex numbers as ordered pairs and their geometric representation, polar form
2. Sets, Functions and Groups: sets and set operations, Venn diagrams, logic (statements, truth tables, quantifiers), relations and functions (into, onto, one-one, inverse), binary operations, groups
3. Matrices and Determinants: matrix types and algebra, determinants and their properties, inverse (adjoint method), row operations (echelon form, rank), systems of linear equations (Cramer's rule, inverse-matrix method, Gauss elimination), homogeneous systems
4. Quadratic Equations: solution by factorisation, completing the square and the quadratic formula; equations reducible to quadratic form; exponential and radical equations; cube roots of unity and fourth roots of unity; remainder and factor theorems, synthetic division; roots and coefficients; nature of roots (discriminant); simultaneous equations
5. Partial Fractions: four cases (distinct linear, repeated linear, irreducible quadratic, repeated quadratic factors)
6. Sequences and Series: arithmetic progression (A.P.), arithmetic means; geometric progression (G.P.), geometric means, infinite geometric series; harmonic progression (H.P.), harmonic means; relation between A, G and H; sigma notation and sums of n, n² and n³; arithmetico-geometric series

8. Mathematical Inductions and Binomial Theorem: principle of induction, binomial theorem for positive integral index (general term, middle term), binomial series for negative or fractional index, approximations
9. Fundamentals of Trigonometry: angles in degrees and radians, arc length (l = rθ), trigonometric ratios, quadrants and signs, identities, values of standard angles
10. Trigonometric Identities of Sum and Difference of Angles: distance formula, fundamental law of trigonometry, compound, double, half and triple angles, sum-to-product and product-to-sum formulas
11. Trigonometric Functions and their Graphs: domain and range, period, even and odd functions, graphs of sin, cos, tan and the others
12. Application of Trigonometry: solving right and oblique triangles, heights and distances, law of sines, law of cosines, law of tangents, half-angle formulas, area of a triangle, circumcircle, incircle and escribed circles (r, R, r₁ r₂ r₃)
13. Inverse Trigonometric Functions: definitions, domain and range, principal values, graphs, addition formulas
14. Solutions of Trigonometric Equations: general solutions, equations reducible to quadratic form, equations in more than one trigonometric function
