For a reaction, ΔH = +40 kJ mol⁻¹ and ΔS = +100 J K⁻¹ mol⁻¹, both roughly constant with temperature. Using ΔG = ΔH − TΔS, above what temperature does the reaction become spontaneous?  
**A) 400 K ✓**  
B) 0.4 K

_Spontaneous when ΔG < 0, i.e. T > ΔH/ΔS = 40 000 J mol⁻¹ ÷ 100 J K⁻¹ mol⁻¹ = 400 K. Not converting kJ to J gives 0.4 K; 127 K confuses 400 K with its Celsius value, about 127 °C._
