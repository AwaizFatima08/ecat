## 0. The most important change for the app

**UET dropped negative marking in 2026.**
- ECAT 2025 (both phases) marked +4 for a correct answer and −1 for a wrong one (About-2025; Ad-2025-I §6a; Ad-2025-II §6a).
- ECAT 2026 marks +4 for a correct answer and 0 for a wrong or blank one: "no passing threshold for any program and no negative marking in ECAT" (About-2026). FAQ-2026 gives the worked example 40 right, 40 wrong, 20 blank = 160/400. The Phase II news release confirms it ("conducted without negative marking"): https://www.uet.edu.pk/newsannouncement/Event/UET-Lahore-Holds-ECAT-2026-Phase-II-Successfully-Across-Punjab-2026.06.23/ [sources/uet-news-ecat-2026-phase2-held-2026-06-23.html]
- Many prep sites still describe −1 marking. The app should use the 2026 rule, keep a setting for −1 marking in case UET brings it back, and re-check when the ECAT 2027 notice appears.

## 2. Subject split per group

UET wording (About-2026, About-2025, Ad-2025-I §6): "Ten questions will be related to English and the rest, i.e., ninety, will equally be divided amongst the other subjects." **Each paper is 10 English + 30 + 30 + 30.** The official stats PDFs label the papers as five groups.

| ECAT group (UET stats label) | Subjects (MCQs) | Marks | Typical intermediate background | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Pre-Engineering | English 10, Mathematics 30, Physics 30, Chemistry 30 | 40 / 120 / 120 / 120 | FSc Pre-Engineering | About-2026; FAQ-2026 |
| ICS (Physics) | English 10, Mathematics 30, Physics 30, Computer Science 30 | 40 / 120 / 120 / 120 | ICS with Physics | same |
| Gen. Science (Statistics, Math, CS) | English 10, Mathematics 30, Statistics 30, Computer Science 30 | same | ICS with Statistics | same |
| Gen. Science (Statistics, Math, Physics) | English 10, Mathematics 30, Statistics 30, Physics 30 | same | General Science | same |
| Pre-Medical | English 10, Biology 30, Physics 30, Chemistry 30 ("Biology in lieu of Mathematics for Pre-medical candidates") | same | FSc Pre-Medical | FAQ-2026 |

Notes:
- FAQ-2026 says "four combinations" and then lists five. Its fifth item is "English, Mathematics, Statistics, Computer Science". Treat it as five.
- **DAE holders** may only take Physics + Mathematics + English + Chemistry, or Physics + Mathematics + English + Computer Science (About-2026; Ad-2025-I §6c). Unlike other candidates, a DAE holder is not tied to the combination they took (Prospectus-2026 §3.1 ii; Determination of Merit note d).
- **Your ECAT combination must match your intermediate subjects.** You are then considered only for programs that fit that combination (Prospectus-2026 §3.1 ii). A-Level students pick the closest combination (Prospectus-2026 §2.2).
- **Which program needs which combination** (Prospectus-2026 p.15 table, Fall 2026; also the Fall 2026 admission advert [sources/uet-ug-admission-advertisement-fall-2026.pdf]):
  - *Engineering programs:* FSc Pre-Engineering or ICS (Maths, Physics, Computer) or equivalent, with 60% in intermediate. Biomedical Engineering (KSK, Narowal) also accepts FSc Pre-Medical.
  - *Architecture / City & Regional Planning / Product & Industrial Design:* as for engineering, and also Maths, Physics and Statistics. Minimum 60% for Architecture and 50% for the other two.
  - *Computing programs (CS, AI, Data Science, Cyber Security, Software Engineering, etc.):* "F.Sc Pre-Engineering/Pre-Medical/ICS (Math, Physics, Statistics & Computer) or equivalent", 50% minimum. Computer Engineering (NCEAC) needs 60%.
  - *Business and Science programs:* no ECAT.
- **Content per subject, in UET's words (FAQ-2026):**
  - English: 10 questions, "comprehension type. A paragraph would be given and candidates would be required to answer multiple choice questions related to the paragraph."
  - Physics: 30 questions, e.g. "Mechanics, Electricity & Magnetism, Work and Energy".
  - Mathematics: 30 questions, e.g. "Differentiation, Integration, Linear Algebra, Trigonometry".
  - Chemistry, CS, Statistics and Biology: 30 questions each, "topics covered in Intermediate / 'A' Level / DAE", requiring candidates to "solve problems by using the skills and concepts learnt".

## 3. Syllabus

### What UET says (official)
- "The Test would be of Intermediate level" (About-2026). The test "covers the entire syllabus of Intermediate/ 'A' Level/ DAE" and the syllabus is not shortened (FAQ-2026).
- **UET publishes no chapter-wise syllabus.** No syllabus PDF exists on ecat.uet.edu.pk or in the admission.uet.edu.pk downloads list (checked 9 Nov 2026), and neither prospectus (2025, 2026) has a syllabus section. Prep sites agree there is "no officially published selective syllabus" **(secondary)**: https://blog.maqsad.io/blog/uet-ecat-syllabus-essential-exam-details, https://www.ilmkidunya.com/ecat/ecat-test-syllabus.aspx
- UET does not name a board. In practice most candidates come from the Punjab boards, so the Punjab textbooks are the working reference. That is our assumption, not something UET says.

### The new Punjab textbooks (National Curriculum of Pakistan 2022–23)
**(secondary)** Every source here is news or a study site; the PCTB/PECTAA site (pctb.punjab.gov.pk) showed a maintenance page on 9 Nov 2026.

  - https://www.mathcity.org/math-11-pectaa (the book is "based on Updated/Revised National Curriculum of Pakistan 2023", PECTAA-approved)

  - https://ahsa.pk/pectaa-issues-new-intermediate-textbooks/
- **What this means for the app (our inference, to confirm):**
  - Candidates for ECAT 2027 (Part II exams in spring 2027) studied the **new Class 11 books** in 2025–26, and probably the **new Class 12 books** in 2026–27.
  - Candidates for ECAT 2026 mostly used the old (2002-curriculum) books.
  - UET has said nothing about a syllabus change. It may simply keep "entire Intermediate syllabus".
  - Plan: tag every question by topic, not by old chapter number. Cover the new-book topics (e.g. complex numbers, special relativity, particle physics, Python) as well as the old ones, and watch the ECAT 2027 notice.

## 4. Merit formula and who uses ECAT

### UET Lahore (Fall 2025 and Fall 2026, unchanged; Prospectus-2026 p.20, Prospectus-2025 "Determination of Merit")

| Candidate type | Weights |
|---|---|
| Intermediate stream | **ECAT % × 33% + FSc Part-I (First Year) % × 50% + Matric % × 17%** |
| O/A Level | ECAT 33% + 11th class (O-level equivalent) 67% |
| DAE | ECAT 33% + sum of 1st and 2nd year % 67% |
| B.Sc. Engineering Technology | ECAT 33% + 7-semester CGPA 67% (2.5/4 = 60%, 4.0 = 90%, interpolated) |
| Non-ECAT programs | Matric 25% + FSc Part-I 75% |

- Minimum aggregate is 50% for any undergraduate program, plus at least 60% in intermediate for Engineering, Architecture and Computer Engineering (NCEAC), and 50% for Science, Technology and Business (Prospectus-2026 p.16, p.20).
- Pre-Medical with additional Maths: the first-year Biology marks are used (Prospectus-2026 §2).
- Hafiz-e-Quran: 0–20 extra marks after an oral test on 17 Jul 2026 (Prospectus-2026 §3).
- Merit uses Part-I marks even if Part-II is complete (FAQ-2026).
- **Example closing aggregates, Fall 2026 Merit List 1, Main Campus Lahore, A1 open merit, morning:** CS 87.47, Electrical 85.14, Mechanical 83.29, Civil 82.64, Chemical 81.70 (https://admission.uet.edu.pk/uploads/downloads/91a868d2-dfcb-4974-af21-453f6b6853d0.pdf) [sources/uet-ug-merit-list-1-min-aggregate-fall-2026.pdf]. Later lists are on https://admission.uet.edu.pk/downloads.
- Fall 2026 drew 21,066 applications with 288,166 program preferences (https://admission.uet.edu.pk/news/43) [sources/admission-uet-news-fall-2026-applications-21066.html].

### Who requires or accepts ECAT
- **Mandate:** UET has run the combined test for Punjab's public engineering institutions since 1999, on the Government of Punjab's direction. Under PEC notifications, "this entrance test is mandatory for admission into undergraduate engineering programs in both public and private engineering institutions" (About-2026). This is UET's statement. A PEC notification itself was not found online in this pass.
- **UET Lahore and its campuses** (Main Lahore, KSK New Campus, Faisalabad, Narowal, RCET Gujranwala): 29 ECAT programs at Lahore alone, including all engineering, CS, AI, Data Science, Cyber Security, Software Engineering, Architecture, CRP, Product Design and Gaming & Animation (https://ecat.uet.edu.pk/General/Programs [sources/ecat-uet-programs-2026-snapshot.html]).
- **UET Taxila:** accepts "TCAT / ECAT / Equivalent acceptable to PEC & UET Taxila"; its FAQ says this test "is conducted by UET Lahore". Merit = **HSSC-I 50% + ECAT 33% + SSC 17%**, the same weights as UET Lahore (https://admissions.uettaxila.edu.pk/Admission_Eligibility.php [sources/uet-taxila-admission-eligibility-fall-2026-snapshot.html]; https://admissions.uettaxila.edu.pk/FAQS.php).
- **ECAT "partner institutes"** (test centres, and in practice users of the Punjab ECAT): BZU Multan, Islamia University Bahawalpur, NFC IET Multan, KFUEIT Rahim Yar Khan, University of Gujrat, UET Taxila, RCET Gujranwala, and UET's Faisalabad and Narowal campuses (https://ecat.uet.edu.pk/General/Partners). The admission rules of each were not checked.
- **University of the Punjab:** accepts ECAT (UET), the NUST test or its own PU tests for its engineering programs **(secondary, 2021)**: https://propakistani.pk/2021/06/18/punjab-university-declares-ecat-mandatory-for-its-engineering-programs/
- **Private universities in Punjab:** PEC requires an entry test from a recognised body. Private universities often accept ECAT or run their own approved test (e.g. UMT's list of accepted tests) **(secondary)**: https://sen.umt.edu.pk/Engineering-Admission-Policy-and-Entry-Test.aspx. Each one must be checked separately.
- **PEC pre-medical route (2024):** pre-medical students may fill up to 40% of engineering seats after a test **(secondary)**: https://propakistani.pk/2024/07/15/pre-medical-students-can-now-enroll-in-engineering-degrees/

## 6. Question style

Official points:
- **Calculators are banned** and rough paper is given (ECAT home). Numerical questions must therefore be solvable by hand in about 1 minute each: 100 questions in 100 minutes, so 60 seconds per question on average.
- **No negative marking from 2026**, so the best strategy is to answer every question. Mocks should teach "never leave a blank" for 2026 rules.
- **English is passage-based reading comprehension** (FAQ-2026). That is 10 MCQs, about 10% of the marks.
- Physics, Maths and the third subject should "require the candidate to solve problems by using the skills and concepts learnt during the intermediate level studies" (FAQ-2026). The emphasis is applied problem-solving, not pure recall.
- The test is CBT: one question per screen, with a mock interface (UET's mock-test demo video https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dA9OjXeAtkE; orientation session before each phase).

Secondary observations (prep sites; not verified against real papers, since UET does not publish past papers):
- Older ECAT English (pre-2015 guideline) had sentence-completion and synonym items. Prep courses still teach synonyms, antonyms and sentence correction (https://www.scribd.com/document/449602397/EntryTestGuidelines-2012; https://gotest.com.pk/ecat-english-test). For 2026 the official description is comprehension only.
- Prep material describes Maths as heavy in calculus, trigonometry and analytic geometry, and Physics as a mix of one-step numerical and conceptual items. There is no official breakdown. The only official hints are UET's examples (differentiation, integration, linear algebra, trigonometry; mechanics, electricity and magnetism, work and energy).
- Pass rate / difficulty: Pre-Engineering averages were 203/400 (Phase I) and 238/400 (Phase II) in 2026 (official stats). A raw score near 50% is typical, so the test is hard; this helps calibrate mock difficulty.

## 8. Conflicts and open questions

1. **Negative marking.** −1 in 2025 and none in 2026 (both official). Many prep sites are out of date. Whatever ECAT 2027 uses is unknown.
2. **2026 date clashes inside UET's own channels.**
   - Phase I test end: 2 Apr or 3 Apr.
   - Phase I result: planned 17 Apr; reported about 11–12 Apr **(secondary)**.
   - Phase II admit card: 10, 14 or 24 Jun.
   - Phase II test: 16–21 or 17–21 Jun.
   - Phase II result: 23 Jun (UET news) vs 30 Jun (admission portal, which is what happened).
3. **2025 Phase II turnout:** 26,803 (UET) vs 26,581 (The News).
4. **Number of combinations.** FAQ-2026 says "four" but lists five. The About page and the stats PDFs show five groups.
5. **Options per MCQ** are not stated anywhere official. Assume 4 but confirm, e.g. from the UET mock video.
6. **Syllabus vs new books.** UET says "entire intermediate syllabus" and lists no chapters. The Punjab NCP books (Class 11 from 2025–26, intermediate revision for 2026) change the topic mix for ECAT 2027 candidates. Chapter lists for the new books are from secondary sites. The old-book lists in §3 are unverified recollection and must be checked against the PTB PDFs.
7. **English content.** Official: comprehension only. Prep sites: synonyms and sentence correction as well. Weight the app towards comprehension.
8. **Rawalpindi centre.** Listed only by NFC IET for 2026 Phase I. UET's lists name Taxila, not Rawalpindi.
9. **PEC mandate.** Known only from UET's wording. The PEC notification itself was not retrieved.
10. **ECAT 2027:** not announced. Re-check ecat.uet.edu.pk and admission.uet.edu.pk/news from January 2027.
